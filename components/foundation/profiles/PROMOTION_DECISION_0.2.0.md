# PPL Profiles 0.2.0 Stable — Promotion Decision

**Decision date:** 2026-08-19  
**Decision:** Promote `0.2.0-rc.3` semantics to **0.2.0 Stable**.

## Why promotion is justified

The Stable gate closes the software/contract questions targeted by the 0.2 line:

- Tutor uncertainty-aware state and directional assessment evidence are deterministic and transaction-safe.
- Broadened real ASSISTments replay covers multiple students, skills, hint use, repeated attempts and bottom-hint patterns.
- A simple independent BKT reference is used only as a sanity baseline; the application gate detects no severe anti-direction anomaly.
- Research explicitly separates conclusion maturity from direction and supports `ready + support`, `inconclusive + mixed`, and `ready + oppose`.
- Strong validation, provenance, independence, rollback and idempotence gates remain explicit.
- Tutor and Research did not expose the same Host-neutral Core/Runtime deficiency; PPL Core 0.3.0 and Runtime 0.1.0 therefore remain frozen.

## What Stable does NOT claim

Stable is a **software contract / reference semantics** decision. It does not claim:

- full-dataset statistical calibration of Tutor mastery probabilities;
- superiority or equivalence to BKT/KT models;
- that Research `ready` is objective scientific truth;
- that a Profile replaces retrieval, experiment execution, fact verification or other Host capabilities.

Those remain application-level empirical validation work and can evolve without breaking the 0.2 contract.
